Trust by design · money

Players see the rules before they pay

Shown on the deposit screen

  • KYC comes first Thibitisha utambulisho kwanza
  • Processing takes 1–24 hours
  • Min TZS 1,000 · Max TZS 5,000,000 per transaction
  • Wagering (x5) applies to bonus money only
  • We never withhold your own deposits or winnings for undisclosed reasons

🧾 Append-only ledger

A database trigger blocks any UPDATE or DELETE on transactions. Balances are derived from the ledger and can be audited.

✅ Official-results settlement

Only the official results provider can settle a bet. Each settlement writes an immutable ledger entry and an audit-log entry.

🛡️ Admin with guardrails

Margins, stake limits and the KYC review queue are managed in the admin panel, and every change is written to the audit log.
